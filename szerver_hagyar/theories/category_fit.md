## How the conversation 'fits' category theory

1. **Objects & Morphisms** — physical quantities and scaling relations.
2. **Functors** — the path-integral Σ maps the free category of paths into ℂ.
3. **Adjunctions** — Free ⊣ Cofree structures discrete/continuous duality.
4. **Dagger-compact** — creation/annihilation are cups/caps (η, ε).
5. **2-/3-categories** — processes and natural transformations = error corrections.
6. **Quotients/Coequalizers** — code space = equalizer of stabilizers; logical ops in quotient.
